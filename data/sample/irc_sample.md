CHAPTER 3 BUILDING PLANNING

SECTION R303 LIGHT, VENTILATION AND HEATING

R303.1 Habitable rooms. Every habitable room needs glazing with an area of at least 8 percent of the room's floor area for natural light. For natural ventilation, the openable part of the windows or doors to the outdoors must be at least 4 percent of the floor area.
Exception: Glazing is not required where artificial light produces an average of 6 footcandles over the room at 30 inches above the floor, and ventilation is not required where a whole-house mechanical ventilation system is installed.

SECTION R304 MINIMUM ROOM AREAS

R304.1 Minimum area. Habitable rooms must have a floor area of not less than 70 square feet. Kitchens are not subject to this minimum.

R304.2 Minimum dimensions. A habitable room must be at least 7 feet in every horizontal dimension. Kitchens are not subject to this minimum.

SECTION R305 CEILING HEIGHT

R305.1 Minimum height. Habitable space, hallways and the portions of basements containing these spaces must have a ceiling height of not less than 7 feet. Bathrooms, toilet rooms and laundry rooms may have a ceiling height of not less than 6 feet 8 inches.
Exception: In rooms with sloped ceilings, at least 50 percent of the required floor area must meet the minimum ceiling height, and floor area where the ceiling is lower than 5 feet is not counted. Beams, girders and ducts may project to within 6 feet 4 inches of the finished floor.

SECTION R308 GLAZING

R308.4 Hazardous locations. Glazing in the following locations is considered hazardous and must be safety glazing: glazing in swinging, sliding and bifold doors; glazing next to a door where the nearest edge is within a 24-inch arc of the door edge and the bottom edge is less than 60 inches above the floor; and glazing in walls or enclosures facing bathtubs and showers where the bottom edge is less than 60 inches above the standing surface.

SECTION R310 EMERGENCY ESCAPE AND RESCUE OPENINGS

R310.1 Emergency escape and rescue opening required. Basements, habitable attics and every sleeping room must have at least one operable emergency escape and rescue opening that opens directly to a public way, yard or court.

R310.2.1 Minimum opening area. Emergency escape and rescue openings must have a net clear opening of not less than 5.7 square feet. Openings at grade floor level may have a net clear opening of not less than 5 square feet. The net clear opening height must be at least 24 inches and the net clear opening width at least 20 inches.

R310.2.2 Window sill height. Where the emergency escape opening is a window, the bottom of the clear opening must be not more than 44 inches above the floor.

SECTION R311 MEANS OF EGRESS

R311.7.1 Stairway width. Stairways must be not less than 36 inches in clear width at all points above the permitted handrail height and below the required headroom height.

R311.7.2 Headroom. The minimum headroom in all parts of the stairway must be not less than 6 feet 8 inches, measured vertically from the sloped line connecting the tread nosings or from the floor surface of the landing.

R311.7.5.1 Risers. The maximum riser height is 7-3/4 inches. Within any flight of stairs, the greatest riser height must not exceed the smallest by more than 3/8 inch.

R311.7.5.2 Treads. The minimum tread depth is 10 inches. Within any flight of stairs, the greatest tread depth must not exceed the smallest by more than 3/8 inch.

R311.7.8.1 Handrail height. Handrail height, measured vertically from the sloped plane adjoining the tread nosing, must be not less than 34 inches and not more than 38 inches.

SECTION R312 GUARDS AND WINDOW FALL PROTECTION

R312.1.1 Where required. Guards are required along open-sided walking surfaces, including stairs, ramps and landings, that are located more than 30 inches measured vertically to the floor or grade below at any point within 36 inches horizontally to the edge of the open side.

R312.1.2 Height. Required guards at open-sided walking surfaces must be not less than 36 inches high measured vertically above the adjacent walking surface.
Exception: Guards on the open sides of stairs may have a height of not less than 34 inches measured from the line connecting the tread nosings.

R312.1.3 Opening limitations. Required guards must not have openings from the walking surface to the required guard height that allow passage of a sphere 4 inches in diameter.

SECTION R314 SMOKE ALARMS

R314.3 Location. Smoke alarms must be installed in each sleeping room, outside each separate sleeping area in the immediate vicinity of the bedrooms, and on each additional story of the dwelling, including basements and habitable attics.

CHAPTER 5 FLOORS

SECTION R502 WOOD FLOOR FRAMING

R502.3 Allowable joist spans. Spans for floor joists must not exceed the values in the joist span tables, which depend on the species and grade of lumber, the joist size and the joist spacing.

R502.6 Bearing. The ends of each joist, beam or girder must have not less than 1-1/2 inches of bearing on wood or metal and not less than 3 inches of bearing on masonry or concrete.

SECTION R506 CONCRETE FLOORS (ON GROUND)

R506.1 General. Concrete slab-on-ground floors must be a minimum of 3-1/2 inches thick.

R506.2.3 Vapor retarder. A 6-mil polyethylene or approved vapor retarder with joints lapped not less than 6 inches must be placed between the concrete floor slab and the base course or prepared subgrade.

CHAPTER 6 WALL CONSTRUCTION

SECTION R602 WOOD WALL FRAMING

R602.3 Design and construction. Exterior walls of wood-frame construction must be designed and constructed in accordance with this section and the fastening schedule, and must be fastened to the floor framing or foundation.

R602.3.1 Stud size, height and spacing. In bearing walls, 2x4 studs supporting a roof and ceiling only may be spaced not more than 24 inches on center. Where 2x4 studs support one floor in addition to the roof and ceiling, the maximum spacing is 16 inches on center. The laterally unsupported height of 2x4 bearing-wall studs is limited to 10 feet. R602.3.2 Top plate. Wood stud walls must be capped with a double top plate installed to provide overlapping at corners and intersections with bearing partitions. End joints in top plates must be offset at least 24 inches.
Exception: A single top plate is permitted where the rafters or joists are centered over the studs with a tolerance of not more than 1 inch.

R602.7 Headers. Headers over openings in bearing walls must be sized according to the header span tables, based on the building width, the ground snow load and the number of floors supported.

R602.8 Fireblocking required. Fireblocking must be provided to cut off concealed draft openings, both vertical and horizontal, in concealed spaces of stud walls and partitions, vertically at the ceiling and floor levels and horizontally at intervals not exceeding 10 feet.

R602.10 Wall bracing. Buildings must be braced in accordance with this section. Braced wall lines are required in each story, and braced wall panels must be located along each braced wall line.

CHAPTER 8 ROOF-CEILING CONSTRUCTION

SECTION R802 WOOD ROOF FRAMING

R802.11 Roof tie-down. Where the uplift force on a rafter or truss exceeds 200 pounds, the rafter or truss must be attached to its supporting wall assembly by connectors capable of resisting the uplift force.

SECTION R806 ROOF VENTILATION

R806.1 Ventilation required. Enclosed attics and enclosed rafter spaces must have cross ventilation for each separate space. Ventilation openings must be protected against the entrance of rain or snow and covered with corrosion-resistant mesh with openings between 1/16 inch and 1/4 inch.

R806.2 Minimum vent area. The minimum net free ventilating area must be 1/150 of the area of the vented space. The ratio may be reduced to 1/300 where at least 40 percent and not more than 50 percent of the required ventilating area is provided by vents in the upper portion of the attic, not more than 3 feet below the ridge.

SECTION R807 ATTIC ACCESS

R807.1 Attic access. Buildings with combustible ceiling or roof construction must have an attic access opening to attic areas that have a vertical height of 30 inches or more and an area of more than 30 square feet. The rough-framed opening must be not less than 22 inches by 30 inches.
